# College Edition – Features

## 1. College, Faculty and Department Management

Provides organizational management for higher
education institutions.

Features:
- College management
- Faculty management
- Department management
- Academic organizational structure

## 2. Semester-Based Academic Management

Supports semester-oriented academic administration.

Features:
- Academic year management
- Semester management
- Academic calendar
- Academic period management

## 3. Student Registration

Supports student registration and academic
record management.

## 4. Course Registration

Supports academic course organization and
student course registration.

## 5. Credit Hour Management

Supports credit-hour-based academic structures
and academic progression.

## 6. Examination and Grading

Supports examination administration and
student academic evaluation.

## 7. GPA and CGPA Calculation

Supports Grade Point Average and Cumulative
Grade Point Average calculations.

## 8. Academic Transcript Management

Supports student academic history and
transcript records.

## 9. Class Scheduling

Supports class and academic timetable
organization.

## 10. Attendance Management

Supports student attendance recording
and monitoring.

## 11. Academic Calendar

Supports the organization of academic dates,
semesters and academic activities.

---

Note: Feature descriptions represent the
customized project scope. Availability and
completion should be verified against the
current application implementation.
